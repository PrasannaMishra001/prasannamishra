<p class="lede">I write the issue before the pull request, so that the reason for a change exists in writing
before the change does. These are all of them.</p>

They come in batches, because that is how I find things: a few days reading one part of the system, then a
list. Fifteen findings about the development environment and CI on one day in February. Five from reading
the API in March. The cron testing issues in June, and the control plane with its sub issues in July.

Not every proposal was accepted, and I think that belongs here too. A request to let organisers delete
contests was declined in favour of archiving them. One of mine was a duplicate of an older issue. One I
closed myself after deciding the approach was wrong. And one I opened by accident and closed the same day.

Seven of these were fixed by other contributors before I got to them.
