A note on the closed ones#

Thirteen of these were closed without merging. I counted why.

Six were pieces I had split out of a larger pull request and then folded back the same day, in late August, before anyone had reviewed them. Their code merged, or is in review, under another number.

Two were absorbed: the pull request stacked on top of each was squash merged first and carried their changes into main.

Two were overtaken by another contributor fixing the same thing first.

One was the original system settings pull request, which was split into four. One I closed and reopened with a proper description. And one I opened from the wrong branch and closed ninety seconds later.

None of them was rejected for what it did.
